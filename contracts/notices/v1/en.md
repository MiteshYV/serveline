# How {{restaurant_name}} uses your details

{{restaurant_name}} needs a few details to take your order. ServeLine runs the ordering system on
the restaurant's behalf and handles your details only as the restaurant instructs.

**What is collected:** your phone number, name and delivery address; what you have ordered before;
and any dietary preference or allergy you tell us. Calls to the restaurant are recorded.

**Why:** to prepare and deliver your order, to keep your order history so that reordering is quick,
to suggest dishes you may like, and — for calls — to check accuracy and improve how the system
understands you.

**How long:** recordings for 90 days. Call transcripts for 12 months, after which your identity is
removed from them. Order records for as long as tax law requires.

**Who sees it:** only {{restaurant_name}} and its staff. Your details are not shared with other
restaurants on ServeLine, and are not sold. Ordering from another ServeLine restaurant means giving
consent again there.

**Your choice:** withdraw consent, or ask for your details to be deleted, at any time — one tap on
the ordering page, or simply tell the restaurant. Deletion is completed within 7 days; past bills
are kept without your name. Withdrawing does not affect an order already being prepared.

Questions or complaints: contact {{restaurant_name}}. You may also complain to the Data Protection
Board of India.
